Measured Sky

Space & planetary science, measured with real data.

Measured Sky is an English-language YouTube channel about space and planetary science. Each video shows the universe's most striking events, built from real public data (for example NASA and JPL datasets) and rendered at a scale your eyes can follow. Sources are named in every video description, and rendered scenes are labeled as rendered.

Watch Measured Sky on YouTube (@measuredsky)

About the Measured Sky publishing tool

Measured Sky uses a private desktop application to prepare videos and publish them to its own YouTube channel. Through YouTube API Services and with the channel owner's authorization, the tool uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and reads the channel's own video and analytics data to check how published videos perform. The tool is used only by the channel owner, on the channel owner's own computer, and only for the Measured Sky channel. It is not offered to the public.

See the Privacy Policy for exactly which Google user data the tool accesses and how it is handled, and the Terms of Use.
